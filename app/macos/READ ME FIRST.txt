FCPXML to AAF  (Mac app, test build v0.5.0)
=========================================

Thanks for testing! This app turns a Final Cut Pro timeline export (FCPXML)
into an AAF for Pro Tools, with the audio embedded, plus clip gain, fades
and crossfades.

Nothing else to install: Python and ffmpeg are built in.
Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.


1. INSTALL (one time, about 1 minute)
-------------------------------------
a) Drag "FCPXML to AAF" into your Applications folder.
   (This matters: macOS won't let it run properly from Downloads.)

b) Open it. This is unsigned test software, so macOS blocks it the first time:

   macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later:
     - Double-click the app. Click "Done" on the warning.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway" next to "FCPXML to AAF". Confirm with your password.

   macOS 14 (Sonoma) and earlier:
     - Right-click the app > Open, then click Open again.

   After that it opens normally with a double-click.


2. TEST 1: THE BUILT-IN SAMPLE (about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------
Click "Try the sample project", then "Export AAF". The AAF lands in
"FCPXML to AAF Sample Export" on your Desktop.

Open it in Pro Tools (File > Open Session, or Import > Session Data).
You should see:
  - 8 tracks: DX 1-4, MX 1-2, FX 1-2
  - Session start 01:00:00:00, 23.976 fps
  - DX 1 + DX 2 at 01:00:00:00: "Interview A001" (two different tones),
    with a short fade-in
  - On DX 1 and DX 2, "Interview A001" crossfades into "Recorder 4ch"
    from 01:00:04:12 to 01:00:05:12
  - "Recorder 4ch" clip gain ramps up from -20 dB to 0 dB
  - Music on MX 1 + MX 2 from 01:00:01:00, clip gain -6 dB, 2-second fade-out
  - FX 1: "SFX Door" at 01:00:13:00 (-3 dB) and "SFX Door 2" at 01:00:23:12
  - FX 2: "SFX Wind" at 01:00:14:00 (-3 dB)
  - Handles: trim a clip outward; about 5 seconds of extra audio is there


3. TEST 2: A REAL EDIT
----------------------
In Final Cut: select a short project, then File > Export XML...
In the app: "Open..." and choose the FCPXML. On the left is your Final Cut
audio, wired to AAF track groups on the right. Fix anything Preflight flags
(right column), then click "Export AAF". By default the AAF goes into an "AAF Export"
folder next to the FCPXML.

Tips:
  - To re-route a role: click it, then click a track group (or use its
    "-> DX" menu). "+ New group" makes a new one, e.g. PFX.
  - Preflight checks every export first. Red items must be fixed (each has
    a fix button); orange ones are worth a look; grey ones are notes.
  - The strip at the bottom shows exactly what the AAF will contain.
  - Save your layout as a preset; it's there next time you open the app.
  - If media has moved, Preflight offers "Locate media..." to find it.

If the AAF won't open in Pro Tools, try again with Timing set to
"Sample-accurate". If fades are missing, set Fades to "Drawn into clip gain".
Both are under Settings. That tells us what Pro Tools prefers.


KNOWN LIMITS (v0.5.0)
---------------------
- Fade shapes (+3 dB, S-curve) arrive as plain linear fades.
- Pan and audio effects (EQ, compression, etc.) are not carried over.
- Retimed (speed-changed) clips are skipped and listed in the report.
- Audio is split into mono tracks (a stereo music clip becomes 2 tracks).
